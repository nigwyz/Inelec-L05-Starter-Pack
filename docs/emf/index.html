How to use a chapter

1 · Read the idea

Each section starts with the geometric picture, then the formula. If you cannot say what the operator measures, the formula will not survive the exam.

2 · Move the visual

Every hard concept has a control. Change the numbers until the readout matches your hand calculation. That is the fastest error check.

3 · Solve before revealing

The quizzes and practice page hide their solutions. Reading a solution is not mastery; producing it from a blank page is.

What the archive actually contains. Controls and finals from 2000 to 2025 (including 2024–25 and 2021–22 with full solutions), recitations from 2013, 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, 2024–25 and 2025, the professor's formula summary, and five lecture chapters. This site is a synthesis; the original papers stay in your course folder and are not republished here.
